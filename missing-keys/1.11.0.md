# Translation handoff: new keys in Hearth & Herald 1.11.0

1.11.0 (The Standing Orders) is the release where the offices start acting on
standing orders, and it adds a Courtesies system for building relations by hand.
New strings cover: the Office of Works (the Exchequer builds your fiefs and grows
your villages), the Muster Rolls (per-fief recruit focus and the Remount Office),
Settlement Instructions and the Extend action, the Chancery (the map-screen board
and drawer), the Thegn (a fourth, martial Clan Commission), and Courtesies (share
a cup, give a gift, or send a courtesy by messenger to build standing with
notables, companions, lords and rulers).

All nine non-English languages should carry a first-pass draft of the settings
strings in this repo, and Russian also covers the game text. If a draft reads
wrong in your language, correct it and open a pull request (or post it on Nexus
or Discord, whatever is easier). Restructure sentences however your language
needs. Terms of art to keep consistent: Chancery (the board), Office of Works,
the Muster / Remount Office, Standing Instructions, Thegn (the field-champion
commission; use your language's closest thegn / huscarl / retainer-champion word
or transliterate), and Courtesy (the relation-building talk or gift).

Two rules that break the game if ignored: keep every {VARIABLE} token exactly
(for example {HERO}, {SETTLEMENT}, {OFFICE}, {REASON}); and do NOT use plural
blocks like {?(X != 1)|s|} - they blank the whole string, so translate the
explicit _1 / _n key pair instead. A few ch_state_* suffix strings begin with a
leading space; keep it.
