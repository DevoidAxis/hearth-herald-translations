# Translation handoff: new keys in Hearth & Herald 1.11.0

1.11.0 (The Standing Orders) is the release where the offices start acting on
standing orders. New strings cover: the Office of Works (the Exchequer builds
your fiefs and grows your villages), the Muster Rolls (per-fief recruit focus
and the Remount Office), Settlement Instructions and the Extend action, the
Chancery (the map-screen board and drawer), and the Thegn (a fourth martial
Clan Commission).

All nine non-English languages carry a first-pass draft of the settings strings
in this repo, and Russian also covers the game text. If a draft reads wrong in
your language, correct it and open a pull request (or post it on Nexus or
Discord, whatever is easier). Same rules as before: keep the {VARIABLES} intact
and restructure sentences however your language needs. A few terms of art to keep
consistent: Chancery (the board), Office of Works, the Muster / Remount Office,
Standing Instructions, and Thegn (the field-champion commission; use your
language's closest "thegn/huscarl/retainer-champion" word or transliterate).
